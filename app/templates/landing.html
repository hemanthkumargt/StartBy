Features

Built around one question: “When should I start?”

Every feature feeds the same calculation, so the answer gets sharper the more you use it.

Start-by time, not just a deadline

Give a task an effort estimate and StartBy works backwards from the due date, with a buffer, to the latest sensible moment to begin.

due − estimate × multiplier × 1.15

Risk radar

Green, amber, red, with a label as well as a colour. A “Do this now” card surfaces the one task that matters.

Adaptive replanning

Missed your start time? Changed the due date? The plan recomputes itself instead of quietly going stale.

Learns your real pace

Per-tag correction multipliers come from actual vs. estimated hours. If study always takes 1.6×, your plan knows.

Smart capture with Gemini

Paste a messy handout, drop a PDF or just type “lab report friday 6pm”. Gemini pulls out tasks and dates, and a local parser takes over if the API is slow, so capture never fails.

Geminiregex + dateparser fallbackPDF text

Voice assistant & notes

Talk to it. Add tasks, ask what's next, or record a note, hands free.

Start-now reminders

A nudge when it's time to begin, sent once, never twice, and retried if a send fails.

Overload warning & report card

See a crunch coming a week ahead, and review how accurate your estimates really were.

How it works

From messy inbox to a calm plan in three steps

  1. 01

    Capture anything

    Type it, say it, paste a syllabus or upload a PDF. Titles, due dates and estimates are filled in for you to confirm.

  2. 02

    See your start-by

    Each task gets a latest-start time and a risk colour. The dashboard puts the most urgent one up front.

  3. 03

    Get nudged, then get better

    A reminder lands when it's time. Finish the task and StartBy learns from how long it really took.
